How a run goes

  1. Land

    Oreivax, a space pirate, beams down to a desert region. Swarms close in from every side.

  2. Collect orbs

    Walk near number and operator orbs and your ship beams them into its processor.

  3. Chain a sum

    Drag across the processor, number, operator, number, and let go.

  4. Strike

    The ship fires where enemies are thickest. The bigger the answer, the bigger the blast.

  5. Bank crystals

    Clear the region, spend crystals on upgrades that last, and take the next one.

Play your way

Every option is in Settings, on the home screen and in the pause menu, and applies at once. Text size and Reduced motion start from your iPhone's own settings.

  • Text size up to 200%Menus, cards and dialogue grow and reflow instead of shrinking to fit.
  • Reduced motionNo screen shake, camera zooms or bobbing. The game plays the same.
  • Flashing effects, screen shake and hapticsEach one switches off on its own. Nothing flashes more than three times a second.
  • Visual cuesNumbers on the health and XP bars, and icons on buttons, so nothing relies on colour alone.
  • Contrast outline and contrast filterCharacters stand out from the sand.
  • Spider filterSpiders are drawn as plain shapes. They behave the same.
  • Camera zoomBrings the view closer for a bigger picture.
