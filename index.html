How a run goes

  1. Land

    Oreivax, a space pirate, beams down to a desert region. Swarms close in from every side.

  2. Collect orbs

    Walk near number and operator orbs and your ship beams them into its processor.

  3. Chain a sum

    Drag across the processor, number, operator, number, and let go.

  4. Strike

    The ship fires where enemies are thickest. The bigger the answer, the bigger the blast.

  5. Bank crystals

    Clear the region, spend crystals on upgrades that last, and take the next one.
