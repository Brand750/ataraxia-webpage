Common questions

How do I play?

Drag on the left side of the screen to move. Your character aims and fires on their own. Walk near the glowing number and operator orbs and your ship beams them into the processor at the bottom right. Drag across the processor, number, operator, number, and let go to call in an air strike. The bigger the answer, the bigger the blast. How to play in the pause menu shows this again.

How do I make the text bigger?

Open Settings, choose the Access tab and move the Text size slider: 100, 125, 150 or 200%. Until you change it, the game follows your iPhone's text size.

Can I turn off flashing, screen shake or vibration?

Yes, each one on its own. Flashing effects and Damage numbers are on the Display tab, Screen shake and Haptics on the Feedback tab. Reduced motion, on the Access tab, also stops camera zooms and bobbing; the game plays the same.

Spiders make me uncomfortable. Can I hide them?

Turn on Spider filter on the Access tab. The spider boss and small spiders are drawn as plain round shapes, including when the boss is defeated. They move and attack the same way.

Where is my progress saved? Can I move it to a new iPhone?

Progress is saved on your iPhone only. There is no account or cloud save, so it can't be moved to another device, and deleting the app deletes it.

How do I start over?

Open Settings, choose the Data tab and tap Reset progress. This erases your upgrades and crystals and can't be undone. Your settings stay.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The game plays fully offline. When you are online it may send anonymous crash and performance reports, as described in the privacy policy.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone with iOS 15 or later. The game is played in landscape.

Does it cost anything?

No. Ataraxia is free, with no in-app purchases and no ads.
