Common questions

How do I play?

Drag on the left side of the screen to move. Your character aims and fires on their own. Walk near the glowing number and operator orbs and your ship beams them into the processor at the bottom right. Drag across the processor, number, operator, number, and let go to call in an air strike. The bigger the answer, the bigger the blast. How to play in the pause menu shows this again.

How do I make the text bigger?

The game follows your iPhone's text size (Settings > Display & Brightness > Text Size on your iPhone), up to 200%. To set a size just for the game, open Settings in the game, choose the Access tab and move the Text size slider. The Reset button next to it goes back to your iPhone's size.

Can I turn off flashing, screen shake or vibration?

Yes, each one on its own. Flashing effects and Damage numbers are on the Display tab, Screen shake and Haptics on the Feedback tab. Reduced motion, on the Access tab, also stops camera zooms, camera shake, shockwaves and blowing sand, and calms particles; the game plays the same. It turns on by itself when Reduce Motion is on in your iPhone's accessibility settings.

Spiders make me uncomfortable. Can I hide them?

Turn on Spider filter on the Access tab. The spider boss and small spiders are drawn as plain round shapes, including when the boss is defeated. They move and attack the same way.

Can I make the game easier to see?

Turn on High contrast on the Access tab. It outlines characters, makes rocks and walls stand out from the ground and dims background art in menus. It turns on by itself when Increase Contrast is on in your iPhone's accessibility settings. Visual cues, on the same tab, adds numbers and icons to the health and XP bars and on/off marks to switches.

Where is my progress saved? Can I move it to a new iPhone?

Progress is saved on your iPhone only. There is no account or cloud save, so it can't be moved to another device, and deleting the app deletes it.

How do I start over?

Open Settings, choose the Data tab and tap Reset progress. This erases your upgrades and crystals and can't be undone. Your settings stay.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The game plays fully offline. When you are online it sends anonymous gameplay statistics, unless you turn them off in Settings > Data, as described in the privacy policy.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone with iOS 15 or later. The game is played in landscape.

Does it cost anything?

No. Ataraxia is free, with no in-app purchases and no ads.
