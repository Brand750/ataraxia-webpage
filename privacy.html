Privacy policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

Ataraxia is a game for iPhone made by Gamekanya Studio (Brandon Nathan Haliman). This page explains what the game does with your information. In short: there is no account, no advertising and no tracking. The only information that leaves your iPhone is anonymous crash and performance data, so we can find and fix problems.

What leaves your iPhone

The game uses Unity's diagnostics service, run by Unity Technologies, the maker of the engine the game is built with. When the game crashes, stops responding or runs badly, it sends Unity:

  • crash logs and error reports
  • performance data, such as frame rate and memory use
  • basic device details, such as the iPhone model and iOS version
  • a random installation ID, so reports from the same install can be grouped

This data is not linked to your name, email or Apple ID. It is not used for advertising or to track you across other apps or websites, and it is not sold. Unity keeps it for up to 90 days. Unity's own policy is at unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy.

What stays on your iPhone

Your game progress and your settings are saved on your iPhone so you can carry on where you left off. They never leave the device and we cannot see them. To erase them, open Settings in the game, go to Data and choose Reset progress, or delete the app.

What we don't collect

We don't ask for or collect your name, email, contacts, location, photos, microphone, camera or any other personal information. The game has no sign-in, no chat, no in-app purchases and no ads.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete the diagnostics data for your installation, or ask any question about this policy, by emailing bnhaliman26@bsd.idserve.net.

Children

Ataraxia is rated 13+ on the App Store. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If what the game collects ever changes, we will update this page and the date at the top before releasing the version that changes it.
