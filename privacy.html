Privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

Ataraxia is a free game for iPhone made by Gamekanya Studio. It has no ads, no in-app purchases and no account, and it plays offline. This page explains what the game does with your information. In short: your progress stays on your iPhone, and the game sends anonymous gameplay statistics so we can see how it is played and make it better. You can turn the statistics off at any time.

What stays on your iPhone

Your game progress and your settings are saved on your iPhone so you can carry on where you left off. They never leave the device and we can't see them. To erase them, open Settings in the game, go to the Data tab and choose Reset progress, or delete the app.

What leaves your iPhone

Unless you turn it off, the game sends anonymous gameplay statistics to PostHog, an analytics service run by PostHog, Inc. The statistics are stored on PostHog's servers in the United States. We use them to understand how people play and to improve the game. The game sends:

  • what happens in the game: starting a run, starting and clearing a region, the wave you reach, a boss appearing and being defeated, picking a Mod, dying, retrying, opening the Workshop, starting and finishing the tutorial, opening the game, and how long you play
  • the game's version and build number, that it runs on iPhone, and whether you are in the tutorial
  • a random ID made for your install of the game, so events from the same install can be grouped

The random ID is not your iPhone's advertising identifier (IDFA). It is not linked to your name, email, Apple ID or any account. If you play offline, the statistics wait on your iPhone and are sent the next time you open the game while online.

PostHog is set to throw away your IP address (your connection's internet address) when the statistics arrive, so no location is stored.

PostHog keeps the statistics for 1 year.

PostHog's own policy is at posthog.com/privacy.

What we don't collect

The game doesn't collect your name, email, contacts, location, photos or advertising identifier. There is no screen recording, no profile of you and no tracking across other apps or websites. We don't sell your data or share it for advertising.

Your choices

Sharing statistics is on when you first install the game. To turn it off, open Settings in the game, go to the Data tab and turn off Share anonymous usage data. Once it's off, nothing more is sent. You can turn it back on at any time.

Because the statistics are anonymous, we can't tell which data belongs to which player, so we can't find or delete a particular player's data. Deleting the app removes the random ID, so anything sent after you reinstall can't be linked to what was sent before.

If you have a question about this policy, email ataraxia.conquest@gmail.com.

Children

Ataraxia is rated 13+ on the App Store and is not made for children under 13.

Changes to this policy

Before we release a version of the game that collects anything new, we will update this page and the date at the top.
